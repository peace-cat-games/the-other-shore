# Shared chronology

The three campaigns share one world. The dates below come from the research notes; the people and their crossings are fiction. Each campaign can change how its own story goes. Where it does, the change stays in that campaign: other campaigns follow the default course below.

## The default course of the shared story

- **The Levins** (Hanna, David, Ruth, Benny) and **the Felds** (Klara, Moritz):
  - They cross on the *Ternen*, Anders Krogh's boat, on the night of Tuesday 5 to Wednesday 6 October, from the beach below Snekkersten.
  - **Lis and Aron Melby** and a few others are on the same boat: twelve or fourteen people in all.
  - They land at Helsingborg harbor before dawn on Wednesday 6 October. Märta Lindqvist registers them and spells LEVIN correctly.
- **Sara Levin** waits at a parsonage in Frederiksberg with Mrs Holst.
  - She crosses on the *Ternen* on the night of Thursday 7 to Friday 8 October with others from the parsonage's network.
  - Wind and the crowded harbor send the boat to Råå, south of Helsingborg, where she lands.
  - A list from Råå reaches Märta with the name spelled "Lewin, Sara, 77", and Märta reunites her with the family on Friday 8 October.
- **Peter Weil** leaves his farm in central Zealand with two other boys from the farm training groups.
  - He crosses farther north around 10 October and lands up the coast from Helsingborg.
  - His name reaches the Helsingborg lists in the days after; in the receiver campaign it may still be unresolved when the campaign ends.
- **Anders Krogh** (rescuer) crosses with his son Poul:
  - first on the night of 3–4 October, with a group of strangers;
  - then on 5–6 October with the Levins;
  - then on 7–8 October with Sara.
  - What he does after that is the rescuer campaign's ending.
- **Märta Lindqvist** (receiver) works from Saturday 2 October to the middle of the month. She meets the Levins on 6 October, Lis Melby's need for a doctor the same morning, and Sara on 8 October.

## What each protagonist can know

- **Hanna** knows her family, her neighbors and helpers in Copenhagen, the doctor and the skipper by name, and Märta, who tells her name at the quay. She never learns Anders's private life.
- **Anders** knows his family, his shore network (Dr Brandt, Erik, Holger), Viggo, and his passengers, but only as much as they tell him: usually a first name, sometimes nothing. He meets Märta only if he lands at Helsingborg and asks someone's name.
- **Märta** knows her family, her colleagues in Helsingborg (Dr Sjöberg, Constable Persson, Mrs Nyström, Folke Berg), and the arrivals, by the names they give or that are written down. She does not know what happened to them in Denmark unless they tell her.
